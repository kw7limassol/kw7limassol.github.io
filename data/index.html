How to read this

Sales
Transactions already registered at the Land Registry.
Deposited
Contracts lodged at the Land Registry — typically off-plan or under construction. A high share here means the market is buying new build, not resale.

The ratio between the two columns tells you more than the total. A nationality with mostly deposited agreements is buying into construction, usually for the residency route.

What this means if you are buying

  1. Pick the district by who your future buyer will be, not by the brochure. Limassol competes for Russian and Israeli money; Paphos for British.
  2. A high deposited share signals a residency-driven market. Those buyers need new build from a licensed developer — resale does not qualify for the €300,000 route.
  3. Price differences between districts are not quality differences. They are demand differences.
  4. These are public figures. Anyone quoting you a market trend without a source is quoting a feeling.
