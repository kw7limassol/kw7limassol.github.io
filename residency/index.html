The mistake that costs a deposit

Resale property does not qualify. Only new build. Only direct from a licensed developer.

Not "nearly new". Not "resale from the first owner". Resale does not qualify under the fast track at all. It is one line in the rules — and it disqualifies most of what a buyer looks at first.

Check eligibility before the deposit, not after.

The signature date changes the number

A reduced 5% VAT rate applies to the first 130 m² of a primary residence, within a value cap. Anything above that is taxed at the standard 19%. The transitional regime runs to 31 December 2026.

Step by step

  1. Confirm the route. New build is the most used path. Check your target property qualifies before anything else.
  2. Prove the income. €50,000 a year from outside Cyprus, documented. This stops more applications than the purchase price does.
  3. Check the developer. The eleven checks are below.
  4. Reservation and legal due diligence. Your lawyer verifies title, encumbrances, permits and the developer itself.
  5. Contract deposited at the Land Registry. The deposit protects you, not the signature.
  6. Payments per schedule, plus VAT. Check whether the reduced rate applies to you.
  7. Application and decision. Roughly six months on the fast track.

Five mistakes we see most often

  • Paying a deposit on a resale apartment, then discovering it does not qualify.
  • Assuming parents are included in the application.
  • Taking a yield figure at face value when it is quoted before tax, management fees and vacancy.
  • Buying a sea view without checking what is permitted to be built in front of it.
  • Signing in January what could have been signed in December.
